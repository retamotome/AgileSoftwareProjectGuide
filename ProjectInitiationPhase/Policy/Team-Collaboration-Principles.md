# Team Collaboration Principles  <br>團隊協作原則    

[![Academy of Management](https://github.com/retamotome/SelfDirectedTraining/raw/main/Management/img/mgt_cover.png)](https://github.com/retamotome/SelfDirectedTraining/blob/main/Management/README.md)  


## 核心理念

Build a team that values truth, efficiency, collaboration, accountability, and continuous improvement.  
建立一個重視真實、效率、協作、當責與持續改善的團隊。  

## Communication Principles｜溝通原則

Build trust through honesty, transparency, and effective information sharing.  
建立信任，從誠實、透明與有效的資訊分享開始。  

* Be Honest and Transparent.   
誠實透明。  
Share facts, opinions, concerns, and risks honestly. Never intentionally conceal or distort information.  
真實表達事實、觀點、疑慮與風險，不得刻意隱瞞或扭曲資訊。  

* Facts Before Assumptions.  
事實優先。  
Clearly distinguish facts, assumptions, and personal opinions. Do not present assumptions as verified facts.  
明確區分事實、假設與個人意見，不得將未經驗證的假設當成事實。  

* Encourage constructive disagreement.  
鼓勵建設性的不同意見。  
Challenge assumptions, evidence, and proposed solutions to find the best outcome for the team.  
針對假設、證據與方案進行討論，以找出對團隊最有利的結果。  

* Share Relevant Information.  
主動分享必要資訊。  
Ensure that information necessary for others to perform their responsibilities is accessible and communicated in a timely manner.  
確保其他成員能及時取得完成工作所需的資訊。  

## Meeting Principles｜開會原則

Meetings are intended to facilitate effective communication, factual discussions, and decision-making.   
會議的目的是促進有效溝通、事實討論與決策。  

Make every meeting purposeful, factual, and action-oriented.  
讓每一次會議都有明確目的，以事實為基礎，並產生具體行動。  

* Speak the Truth, No Nonsense, No Acting.  
講真話，不講廢話，不要演戲。  
Communicate honestly and directly. Avoid meaningless discussions, unnecessary embellishments, political games, or performative behavior.  
誠實且直接地表達，不要進行沒有意義的討論、不必要的包裝、政治操作或表演式溝通。  

* Record, Verify, and Report.  
確實記錄、會後查證、下次回報。   
If you do not understand something or are uncertain about any information, explicitly record it during the meeting. Follow up after the meeting to investigate and verify the facts, then provide the verified results at the next meeting. Never pretend to know something you do not.  
對於不了解或不確定的事項，應於會議中確實記錄，並於會後進行調查與驗證，在下次會議提供經查證的真實結果。絕不可假裝自己知道答案。  

* Every Meeting Must Have a Purpose.  
每場會議都應有明確目的，避免沒有必要的會議。  

* Decisions and Action Items Must Be Clear.  
會議結束前，應確認決策、負責人及後續行動。  


## Decision-Making Principles｜決策原則

Best Ideas Win, Not the Loudest Voice.  
最好的觀點勝出，而不是聲音最大的人勝出。  

Seek consensus first, use majority vote when necessary, and commit to decisions as a team.  
優先尋求共識，必要時採多數決，並由團隊共同承擔決策結果。  

* Evidence-Based Decisions.   
依證據做出決策。  
Decisions should be based on facts, evidence, technical feasibility, and business requirements.  
依據事實、證據、技術可行性及商業需求做決策。

* Best Solution Over Personal Preference.  
最佳方案優先。  
Choose the solution that best satisfies the overall objectives and constraints, regardless of who proposed it.  
無論是誰提出的方案，都應以整體目標及限制條件來評估。  

* Decouple Problems Before Solving Them.   
解決問題前先進行觀念解耦。  
Challenge existing assumptions and constraints. Explore alternative perspectives before optimizing the current approach.  
重新檢視既有假設與限制，在最佳化現有方案之前，先探索其他可能性。  

* Consensus First.  
優先尋求共識。    
Decisions should be made through open discussion and mutual understanding. The goal is to reach a shared direction, not to impose personal preferences or rely solely on hierarchical authority.  
決策應透過公開討論與相互理解，優先尋求團隊共識，而不是強加個人偏好或單純依賴職位權威。  

* Understand Before Agreeing.  
瞭解後再表態。  
Everyone should have an opportunity to express opinions, raise concerns, and understand the reasoning behind proposed decisions.  
每個人都應有機會表達意見、提出疑慮，並瞭解決策背後的理由。  

* Majority Vote When Consensus Cannot Be Reached.  
無法達成共識時採多數決。    
If reasonable discussion does not lead to consensus, the team shall make the decision through a majority vote. Once decided, the team should respect the outcome and move forward together.  
若經過充分且合理的討論仍無法達成共識，則由團隊採多數決決定。決議形成後，團隊應尊重結果並共同推進。  

* Consensus Does Not Mean Unanimity.  
共識不等於全員完全認同。     
Consensus does not require everyone to consider a decision perfect. It means concerns have been heard, trade-offs are understood, and the team is willing to move forward with the decision.  
共識並不代表每個人都認為決策完美，而是所有人的疑慮都已被聽見、取捨已被理解，且團隊願意共同執行。  

* Decisions Must Be Actionable.  
決策必須能夠執行。  
Every decision must have clear ownership, responsibilities, and follow-up actions. Decisions should not remain unresolved discussions.  
每項決策都必須有明確的負責人、責任分工與後續行動，不應讓討論停留在沒有結論的狀態。  

## Overtime Policy｜禁止加班原則

Build better processes and systems instead of relying on longer working hours.  
透過改善流程與系統解決問題，而不是依賴延長工作時間。  

Overtime is NOT a demonstration of dedication or productivity. It is considered an indication of poor performance, inefficient work management, or inadequate resource utilization, resulting in unnecessary waste of company resources.  
加班並不代表敬業或工作效率高。加班將被視為績效不彰、工作管理效率不佳或資源運用不當的表現，亦代表公司資源遭到不必要的浪費。  

* No Routine Overtime.   
禁止常態性加班。   
All tasks should be properly planned, prioritized, and completed within regular working hours.  
所有工作應妥善規劃、排定優先順序，並於正常工作時間內完成。  

* Fix the Process, Not the Hours.   
改善流程，而不是延長工時。   
When tasks cannot be completed within regular working hours, identify and address the root causes, such as poor estimation, inefficient processes, unclear requirements, or inadequate resource allocation. Do not use overtime as a substitute for effective management.  
當工作無法於正常工作時間內完成時，應找出並解決根本原因，例如估時不準、流程效率不佳、需求不明確或資源配置不當。不得以延長工時取代有效的管理。  

* Overtime Indicates a Potential Systemic Problem.  
加班應視為績效或管理異常的警訊，而不是敬業程度的證明。  

* Exceptional Situations Are Not the Norm.  
重大緊急事故可以依適用法令及授權程序處理，但必須事後檢討，避免成為常態。  


## Ownership & Accountability｜責任與當責

Take responsibility, communicate risks early, and be accountable for outcomes.  
主動承擔責任，及早揭露風險，並對執行結果負責。  

* Take Ownership.   
承擔責任。  
Take responsibility for assigned tasks and their outcomes, not merely the activities performed.  
對交付的工作及成果負責，而不只是完成被交辦的動作。  

* Raise Risks Early.   
儘早提出風險。  
Identify and communicate potential risks, blockers, and delays as early as possible.  
儘早提出風險、阻礙及可能的延誤，不要等到問題無法挽回才回報。  

* Do Not Hide Problems.   
不隱瞞問題。  
Problems should be exposed and addressed, not concealed to protect personal performance records.  
應主動揭露並處理問題，不應為了維護個人績效紀錄而隱瞞。  

* Authority Must Match Responsibility.   
權責必須相符。  
Responsibilities must be accompanied by sufficient authority, resources, and information.  
承擔責任的人，應同時擁有足夠的權限、資源及資訊。  

## Continuous Improvement｜持續改善

Continuously improve the system through learning, feedback, and simplification.   
透過學習、回饋與簡化，持續改善系統與工作方式。  

* Improve the System, Not Just Individual Performance.   
改進系統，而不僅僅是個人表現。   
Identify and eliminate systemic inefficiencies rather than relying solely on individual effort.  
找出並消除系統性效率問題，而不是只依賴個人努力。   

* Learn from Failures.   
從失敗中學習。  
Treat failures as opportunities to understand root causes and prevent recurrence.  
分析根本原因，建立預防措施，避免問題重複發生。  

* Avoid Unnecessary Complexity.   
避免不必要的複雜化。  
Prefer simple, maintainable, and effective solutions over unnecessary complexity.  
優先選擇簡單、可維護且有效的方案。  

* Measure Actual Outcomes.   
衡量實際結果。  
Evaluate improvements based on measurable results and real-world impact, not perceived activity or effort.  
評估改進措施時，以實際成果衡量，而不是以主觀感受到的行動表現或投入時間判斷成效。
